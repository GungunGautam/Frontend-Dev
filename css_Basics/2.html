Rich History

Paris has been a major European city for over 2,000 years, with its origins dating back to the 3rd century BC when it was a settlement of the Parisii tribe. Throughout centuries, it has been the capital of powerful kingdoms and empires, shaping world history through art, culture, and politics.

From the medieval Notre-Dame Cathedral to the revolutionary storming of the Bastille, Paris has witnessed and influenced countless historic moments that changed the course of civilization.

Must-See Attractions

Eiffel Tower

The iconic iron lattice tower stands 330 meters tall and offers breathtaking views of the entire city. Built in 1889, it has become the global symbol of France.

Louvre Museum

Home to thousands of works of art, including the Mona Lisa and Venus de Milo, the Louvre is the world's largest art museum and a historic monument in Paris.

Vibrant Culture

Paris is renowned as a global center for art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture. The city has been home to countless artists, writers, and thinkers who have shaped modern culture. From impressionist masterpieces to haute couture fashion shows, Paris continues to set trends worldwide.

The café culture, street performances, and world-class theaters create an atmosphere of creativity and sophistication that captivates millions of visitors each year.

Exquisite Cuisine

French cuisine is celebrated worldwide, and Paris offers the finest gastronomic experiences. From buttery croissants and delicate macarons to coq au vin and bouillabaisse, every dish is a work of art.

The city boasts numerous Michelin-starred restaurants, charming bistros, and bustling markets where you can savor authentic French flavors and culinary traditions passed down through generations.
